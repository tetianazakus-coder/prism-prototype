08 / A few things to know

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is the trial really free?+

Yes. 14 days of Accelerator, no card. When it ends you move to Essential and keep your deals.

What is the revenue share?+

All plans include revenue sharing as set out in the Broker Agreement, with different rates for deals you source and leads we send you.

What does Pro add?+

House-sourced borrower leads, Lender Spotlight, Broker Spotlight and a public listing, a team-prepared branded deal memo and phone support.

How does territory exclusivity work?+

It's an add-on for Pro brokers, priced per territory. Talk to us to check availability.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. Cancel from Plan & billing; your deals stay saved.

Is LenderPrism a lender?+

No. We're a platform and brokerage network that connects your deals with lenders.
