Header and Brokers hero: the shortlist

Two ways to organise the header menu, and one change to the Brokers hero. The hero change works with either menu. Option 1 is built in the prototype; option 2 is the menu the site had before; option 3 is a sketch.

Why these

Your request

Take Pricing out of the header on Home, the lender pages and the borrower pages, so the site feels like a brokerage. Keep it for brokers.

Research

Keep the main menu the same on every page. Both menus below do: Pricing sits inside a dropdown, never as a top-level item.

Buttons

Your labels on every page: Start free trial for brokers, Submit a deal for borrowers, Create free profile for lenders. Home and About show our Get started.

Option 1: Brokers dropdown

Brokers ▾ · Borrowers · Lenders · About on every page. The Brokers menu holds For Brokers and Plans and pricing; the other three are plain links.

ForAll three audiences are visible without a click. Pricing sits under the audience it belongs to. The menu is the same on every page. Fits on one row down to 860 wide.
AgainstBrokers opens a menu while Borrowers and Lenders go straight to a page. Reaching the Brokers page takes two clicks. Find an advisor leaves the header (Home, the Borrowers page and the footer still link to it).

Option 2: Solutions dropdown

Solutions ▾ · About on every page. The Solutions menu holds all three audiences, Find an advisor, and Plans and pricing as a small link under For Brokers.

Brokers, Solutions open
ForEvery menu item behaves the same way. Each audience has a one-line description. Find an advisor stays in the header. The site had it before, so going back is quick.
AgainstThe audiences are hidden behind one click, on Home too. “Solutions” is SaaS vocabulary, which is the feel you want less of.

Option 3: Brokers hero with two buttons

The trial stays the filled button. “See pricing” becomes an outlined second button. “Book a call” moves into the small line under them. It needs no Brokers dropdown, so it is shown with a plain menu: Brokers · Borrowers · Lenders · About.
