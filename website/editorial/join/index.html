About the opportunity

We are building a next-generation capital markets platform designed for experienced multifamily and commercial real estate mortgage brokers who want to operate at an institutional level, without the bureaucracy of traditional firms.

This is an opportunity for proven producers to increase deal flow, improve execution and significantly grow income through a combination of high commission splits, analytical support, marketing, promotion and house-sourced opportunities.

Who this is for

Strictly for experienced commercial mortgage brokers who

  • Have at least 2 years of direct multifamily and CRE lending or brokerage experience
  • Are comfortable working in a 100% commission-based environment
  • Want to scale production with better infrastructure and support
  • Understand how to source, structure and close commercial debt transactions

Required transaction experience, at least one of

  • Multifamily · Student housing · MHC
  • Retail
  • Industrial · Warehouse · Self-storage
  • Office and medical office
  • Hotel · Motel
  • Land, development or construction
  • Seniors housing

What you'll do

  • Originate and close commercial real estate debt transactions
  • Source new borrower relationships and maintain an active pipeline
  • Work with borrowers to structure financing requests
  • Collaborate with internal analytical and execution teams to size loans, package deals, present opportunities to lenders, and manage transactions from initial inquiry through closing
  • Maintain professional communication with lenders and capital providers

What you'll get

  • High commission splits, well above traditional platforms
  • Participation in house-sourced deals
  • Analytical and underwriting support to improve execution quality
  • Access to a broad lender network: banks, agencies, debt funds and more
  • A centralized capital markets process that enhances credibility with lenders
  • Tools and infrastructure to help you scale beyond your current production level

Compensation

  • 100% commission-based
  • Above-market commission splits
  • Additional income opportunities through platform-generated deal flow

What you must bring

  • Proven ability to self-source business
  • Strong understanding of CRE loan structures and capital stacks
  • Existing or developing network of borrowers
  • High level of professionalism and accountability
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with a desire to build a long-term book of business
