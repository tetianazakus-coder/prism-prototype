Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is LenderPrism?+

LenderPrism is a technology-enabled commercial mortgage brokerage offering lender access, research, deal management tools, and execution support for commercial mortgage brokers.

Which plan should I choose?+

Essential provides foundational access for one active deal. Accelerator adds capacity for ongoing lender research and outreach. Pro adds broker promotion, more research capacity, and execution support.

Can I try a paid plan?+

Yes. Accelerator and Pro are available for a 14-day free trial.

What are lender contact reveals?+

A contact reveal gives you access to lender contact information held within LenderPrism. Your monthly allowance depends on your plan.

What are Prospector research credits?+

Prospector credits are used for bank contact research. Accelerator includes 25 credits per month, and Pro includes 100. Additional credits are available for purchase.

Does a subscription include borrower leads?+

No. House leads are not included in Essential, Accelerator, or Pro. However, the Pro plan allows brokers to create a searchable profile for borrowers to learn more about you and to connect with you. Connections made here are subject to a split with LenderPrism as detailed in the Broker Agreement entered into with LenderPrism.

What does Pro execution support include?+

Sizing and preparation of a lender-ready deal memo. May also include lender outreach. We discuss the assignment and establish the scope of support before work begins. Transaction compensation is detailed in the Broker Agreement entered into with LenderPrism. Any services or charges outside your subscription are identified separately.

Does my subscription determine my commission split?+

No. Subscription features and transaction compensation are separate. Any applicable transaction compensation is established in your Broker Agreement entered into with LenderPrism.

Can I explore an independent originator affiliation?+

Yes. If you are interested in originating through LenderPrism, book a conversation (opens in a new tab) about the separate affiliation opportunity and its requirements.
