Our story

Better coordination. Greater transparency. Stronger execution.

Tony TalamasFounder

Commercial real estate financing depends on relationships, sound judgment, and the ability to execute. Yet the process often involves scattered information, disconnected communication, and limited visibility into which lenders are the right fit.

LenderPrism was founded to bring that process together.

Built on more than 20 years of experience in commercial real estate lending and mortgage brokerage, LenderPrism connects industry expertise with technology to help borrowers and brokers move from financing need to closing with greater clarity and confidence.

For borrowers, that means a more organized financing experience: connecting with experienced professionals, exploring suitable lending options, and staying informed as a transaction progresses.

For independent brokers, it means the infrastructure to support their relationships and expertise—with tools to identify lenders, manage outreach, compare quotes, organize transactions, and collaborate with clients.

Our mission is straightforward: bring better coordination, transparency, and execution to commercial real estate financing, helping borrowers and brokers build stronger relationships and work more efficiently.

What we believe

  1. 01

    People lead the process.

    Relationships, judgment, and follow-through matter. Technology helps organize the work; a broker guides the financing strategy and coordinates the assignment.

  2. 02

    Coordination improves execution.

    Clear responsibilities, organized information, and targeted lender outreach help borrowers, brokers, and lenders work through the financing process.

  3. 03

    Clarity builds confidence.

    Borrowers and brokers should understand where an assignment stands, what comes next, and which decisions require attention.
