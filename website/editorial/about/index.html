Our story

Hi, I'm Tony Talamas. For over 20 years, I've worked as a commercial mortgage broker and direct multifamily lender. In that time, I've seen lenders come and go. Underwriting guidelines change. Loan officers switch shops. Many lenders have different appetites from one year to the next—sometimes quarterly.

Keeping track of active lenders and their changing guidelines is time consuming. It pulls you away from what you do best. So I set out to do something completely different. I'm improving the way multifamily and CRE professionals identify lenders.

Tony TalamasFounder
